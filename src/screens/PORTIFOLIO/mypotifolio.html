// About

One person, several
products, no committee.

I'm Innotec — an independent developer running multiple software products at once across mobile, web, and blockchain. No team to route decisions through, no roadmap meetings: I scope it, build it, and ship it.

My workflow leans hard on AI coding agents — Cursor, Windsurf, Claude — not to replace the thinking, but to compress the distance between an idea and a working build. Strategy, architecture, and product decisions stay mine; the tooling just removes the friction.

Most of what I build sits at the intersection of underserved problems and emerging tech — coffee traceability for smallholder farmers, on-device AI fitness coaching, practical tools people actually open every day.
